02 / BEHIND THE WORK

A curious mind.
A maker’s instinct.

A little about the person behind the projects.

Who I Am

I am a Programmer & Full‑Stack Developer, uniting creative, data‑driven, and full‑stack programming styles to build impactful solutions. I thrive at the intersection of design and engineering — crafting experiences that are as thoughtful as they are functional, and turning complex problems into clean, purposeful code.

What I Build

I develop full-stack and data-driven applications using Python, Java, JavaScript, and SQL. I work with large datasets (10,000+ rows), leveraging Power BI, Talend, and enterprise systems to automate workflows, optimize performance, and transform raw data into strategic insights.

How I Work

I approach complex challenges methodically—breaking them into manageable components and solving them with clean, maintainable code. I prioritize scalability, performance, and clarity, using version control, structured documentation, and iterative testing to ensure quality from development to deployment.

What Drives Me

I’m motivated by building systems that create clarity from complexity—whether turning unstructured data into meaningful insight or engineering features that improve real-world workflows. Continuous learning and technical growth are central to how I evolve as a developer.

Fun Facts

Outside of development, I’m passionate about gaming—especially platformers, RPGs, and increasingly horror titles. Game design was my original gateway into programming, and that creative foundation still influences how I approach technology today.

Certifications

I hold multiple industry-recognized certifications that strengthened my expertise in software development, data systems, and modern digital workflows—reinforcing both my technical foundation and enterprise readiness.
